美股流动性与机构仓位报告

口径与数据说明

净流动性

  • = 美联储总资产 − 财政部 TGA 账户 − 隔夜逆回购（RRP）
  • 市场常用的近似口径，衡量美联储和财政部带来的美元流入流出
  • 与股市中期走势相关，但不是因果定律

取数口径

  • 美联储资产、TGA：每周三数值（H.4.1，周四公布）
  • 逆回购、HY 利差：当周日均值
  • VIX、标普500、纳指100：当周最后一个交易日收盘

指数与 ETF

  • 标普500、纳指100 用 FRED 的指数点位代表 SPY、QQQ
  • 涨跌幅与 ETF 基本一致，但不含分红
  • SPY 价格约为标普500 的十分之一，点位不能直接对比

高收益债利差

  • ICE BofA 美国高收益债指数的期权调整利差（OAS）
  • 利差走阔 = 信用市场要求更高的风险补偿
  • FRED 只提供最近 3 年的数据

VIX

  • 标普500 期权隐含的未来 30 天年化波动率
  • <15 平静 · 15–20 正常 · 20–30 升温 · 30–35 紧张 · >35 恐慌

NFCI 金融状况指数

  • 芝加哥联储编制，综合 100 多个金融市场指标
  • 0 = 历史平均；正值偏紧、负值偏松
  • 芝加哥联储每周会修订历史值，本页随之更新

CFTC 期货持仓

  • 金融期货持仓报告（TFF），每周五公布截至周二的数据
  • 净多头 = 多头 − 空头，再除以未平仓量
  • 资产管理人偏长线；杠杆基金常用期货对冲现货，长期为负属正常
  • 3、6、9、12 月中旬换月那周会出现一次性跳水，属移仓噪音

自动更新

  • 每周六北京时间 21:17 自动取数（周日再跑一次补漏）
  • 每次从 2018 年起整段重取，与源头最新版本一致
  • 任何一项数据取不到或异常，本周不更新，不插值、不补数

Methodology & data notes

Net liquidity

  • = Fed total assets − Treasury General Account (TGA) − overnight reverse repo (RRP)
  • A common market proxy for dollar liquidity added or drained by the Fed and the Treasury
  • Correlated with equities over months, but not a causal law

Data basis

  • Fed assets, TGA: Wednesday levels (H.4.1, released Thursday)
  • RRP, HY spread: weekly average
  • VIX, S&P 500, Nasdaq-100: last close of the week

Indices vs ETFs

  • S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 index levels from FRED stand in for SPY and QQQ
  • Moves match the ETFs closely, excluding dividends
  • SPY trades near one tenth of the index, so levels are not comparable

High-yield spread

  • Option-adjusted spread of the ICE BofA US High Yield Index
  • Wider spread = credit markets demand more compensation for risk
  • FRED provides only the last 3 years

VIX

  • 30-day implied volatility of S&P 500 options, annualized
  • <15 calm · 15–20 normal · 20–30 elevated · 30–35 stressed · >35 panic

NFCI

  • Chicago Fed National Financial Conditions Index, built from 100+ market measures
  • 0 = historical average; positive = tighter, negative = looser
  • The Chicago Fed revises history weekly; this page follows

CFTC positioning

  • Traders in Financial Futures report, released Friday for Tuesday positions
  • Net long = long − short, divided by open interest
  • Asset managers are longer-term; leveraged funds often hedge cash longs with futures, so a net short is normal
  • Roll weeks in mid Mar / Jun / Sep / Dec show one-off drops (noise)

Auto update

  • Every Saturday 13:17 UTC (retry on Sunday)
  • The full history since 2018 is re-fetched each time to match the latest source vintage
  • If any source fails or looks wrong, nothing is updated that week; gaps are never filled
