指标说明与使用方法
  • 净流动性：市场上常用的近似口径，跟踪美联储资产负债表扩张或缩减、财政部发债或花钱带来的美元流入流出。它与股市的中期走势有相关性，但不是因果定律。逆回购余额已经接近 0，它现在基本不再起缓冲作用，变化主要来自美联储资产和 TGA。
  • HY OAS：ICE BofA 美国高收益债指数的期权调整利差，是信用市场的风险偏好温度计。
  • NFCI：芝加哥联储金融状况指数，综合 100 多个金融市场指标；0 为历史平均，正值偏紧、负值偏松。芝加哥联储每周会修订历史值。
  • 百分位：当前值在最近 156 周（3 年）中低于它的数值所占比例。
  • COT：每周五公布截至周二的持仓，滞后 3 天；只覆盖期货，看不到现货和场外衍生品，不代表机构整体买卖。
  • 怎么用：慢变量定方向（净流动性趋势、利差、仓位极端值），不要拿单周数据择时。仓位处于 3 年高位时，下跌中被动减仓的空间更大；处于低位时，反弹燃料更多。
  • 自动更新：每周六北京时间 21:17 自动从 2018 年起整段重取（周日再跑一次补漏）；任何一项数据取不到或异常，当周不更新，不插值、不补数。
  • 数据来源：FRED（WALCL、WTREGEN、RRPONTSYD、BAMLH0A0HYM2、NFCI、VIXCLS、SP500、NASDAQ100）与 CFTC 公开报告（E-mini S&P 500 代码 13874A，Nasdaq Mini 代码 209742），2018 年以来的周度数据。
  • Net liquidity: a common market proxy for dollar liquidity added or drained by the Fed's balance sheet and Treasury issuance and spending. It correlates with equities over months but is not a causal law. Reverse repo is now near zero and no longer acts as a buffer; changes come mainly from Fed assets and the TGA.
  • HY OAS: option-adjusted spread of the ICE BofA US High Yield Index, a gauge of credit risk appetite.
  • NFCI: Chicago Fed National Financial Conditions Index, built from 100+ market measures; 0 = average, positive tighter, negative looser. History is revised weekly.
  • Percentile: share of the last 156 weeks (3 years) below the current value.
  • COT: released Friday for Tuesday positions (3-day lag); futures only, so it does not show cash or OTC positions and is not total institutional buying or selling.
  • How to use: let the slow variables set direction (net liquidity trend, spreads, positioning extremes); do not time trades on a single week. Near a 3-year high in positioning there is more room for forced selling in a decline; near a low, more fuel for a rebound.
  • Auto update: every Saturday 13:17 UTC the full history since 2018 is re-fetched (retry on Sunday); if any source fails or looks wrong, nothing is updated that week and gaps are never filled.
  • Sources: FRED (WALCL, WTREGEN, RRPONTSYD, BAMLH0A0HYM2, NFCI, VIXCLS, SP500, NASDAQ100) and CFTC public reports (E-mini S&P 500 code 13874A, Nasdaq Mini code 209742), weekly since 2018.
