指标说明与使用方法

净流动性

= 美联储总资产 − 财政部 TGA − 隔夜逆回购。衡量美联储和财政部带来的美元流入流出。与股市中期走势相关，但不是因果定律。逆回购已接近 0，变化主要来自美联储资产和 TGA。

HY 利差

ICE BofA 美国高收益债指数的期权调整利差，是信用市场的风险偏好温度计。走阔 = 信用市场要求更高的风险补偿。FRED 只提供最近 3 年。

NFCI

芝加哥联储金融状况指数，综合 100 多个金融市场指标。0 为历史平均，正值偏紧、负值偏松。历史值每周会被修订。

CFTC 持仓

每周五公布截至周二的持仓，滞后 3 天。只覆盖期货，看不到现货和场外衍生品，不代表机构整体买卖。换月那周的一次性跳水是移仓噪音。

百分位

当前值在最近 156 周（3 年）中低于它的数值所占比例。100 表示 3 年最高，0 表示 3 年最低。

怎么用

慢变量定方向：净流动性趋势、利差、仓位极端值。不要拿单周数据择时。仓位处于 3 年高位时下跌中被动减仓空间更大，低位时反弹燃料更多。

自动更新

每周六北京时间 21:17 自动从 2018 年起整段重取，周日再跑一次补漏。任何一项数据取不到或异常，当周不更新，不插值、不补数。

SOFR − IORB

隔夜担保融资利率减去美联储给银行准备金的利率。平时为负或接近 0；明显转正说明银行间缺钱，2019 年 9 月回购危机时单日飙到 +315 基点。看单日最高比看均值更早发现压力。

数据来源

FRED：WALCL、WTREGEN、RRPONTSYD、BAMLH0A0HYM2、NFCI、VIXCLS、SP500、NASDAQ100、SOFR、IORB（2021 年 7 月前用 IOER）。CFTC：E-mini S&P 500（13874A）、Nasdaq Mini（209742）。

Net liquidity

= Fed total assets − Treasury General Account − overnight reverse repo. Dollar liquidity added or drained by the Fed and the Treasury. Correlated with equities over months, but not a causal law. RRP is near zero, so changes come mainly from Fed assets and the TGA.

HY spread

Option-adjusted spread of the ICE BofA US High Yield Index, a gauge of credit risk appetite. Wider = credit markets demand more compensation. FRED provides only the last 3 years.

NFCI

Chicago Fed National Financial Conditions Index, built from 100+ market measures. 0 = average, positive = tighter, negative = looser. History is revised weekly.

CFTC positioning

Released Friday for Tuesday positions (3-day lag). Futures only — no cash or OTC positions, so not total institutional buying or selling. One-off drops in roll weeks are noise.

Percentile

Share of the last 156 weeks (3 years) below the current value. 100 = 3-year high, 0 = 3-year low.

How to use

Let slow variables set direction: net liquidity trend, spreads, positioning extremes. Do not time trades on a single week. Near a 3-year positioning high there is more room for forced selling; near a low, more fuel for a rebound.

Auto update

Every Saturday 13:17 UTC the full history since 2018 is re-fetched, with a retry on Sunday. If any source fails or looks wrong, nothing is updated that week; gaps are never filled.

SOFR − IORB

Secured overnight funding rate minus the rate the Fed pays on bank reserves. Normally negative or near zero; a clearly positive spread means banks are short of cash — it hit +315 bp on one day in the September 2019 repo squeeze. The weekly high flags stress earlier than the average.

Sources

FRED: WALCL, WTREGEN, RRPONTSYD, BAMLH0A0HYM2, NFCI, VIXCLS, SP500, NASDAQ100, SOFR, IORB (IOER before July 2021). CFTC: E-mini S&P 500 (13874A), Nasdaq Mini (209742).
